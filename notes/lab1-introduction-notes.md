1. What I Did (Objective)
The lab is about completing a network diagram to understand the different devices used within a network and what type of cable connects them.

2. Why I Did It (Scenario Fit)
Different devices pair requires different cabling depending on the layer or the devices it connects to. 

3. How I Did It (Method Used)
I have used a straight through UTP cable for different devices connection. PC1 and PC2 to switch, switch to router, router to firewall. But for connecting 2 routers, I used a crossover since they are using the same pins where the data transmits and receives.

4. What I Learned
Understanding the basics of devices such as the PC, switch, firewall, router, servers and how to connect each one and when to use the T568A and T568B wiring standard. 

5. Gaps / Struggles
Sometimes I get confused when to use the straight through and crossover cables. 

6. Industry Best Practices
A network is built through physical connection which is the layer 1 in OSI model, having this knowledge will always be useful for troubleshooting and network designs.
